# ForeverRoute Community

Community hub for players of **World of Warcraft: Forever** using **ForeverRoute**.

**[Open the forum / Discussions](https://github.com/Deciwarrz/ForeverRoute-Community/discussions)** · **[Report a bug](https://github.com/Deciwarrz/ForeverRoute-Issues/issues/new)** · **[Download the addon](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/foreverroute)**

## What belongs here

- **Shared Routes / Sdílení rout** — share original leveling routes, quest order and tips for specific race/class/level bands.
- **Q&A** — ask other players about quest objectives and route choices.
- **Ideas** — suggest new features and improvements.
- **General** — discuss WoW Forever and ForeverRoute.
- **Announcements** — official community updates.

**Bug reports and broken pins belong in [ForeverRoute-Issues](https://github.com/Deciwarrz/ForeverRoute-Issues/issues/new), not the community forum.** The ForeverRoute source repository remains private.

```md
Solo or group:
Zones:

```

**Status:** Portable FRR1 route import/export in Control Center and the in-game addon is **in development**. Until it is tested, describe the route or share screenshots, not executable code.

## Community rules

1. Be respectful. No harassment, spam, or impersonation.
2. Share only original routes and imagery you are entitled to redistribute. Credit route authors; do not reupload proprietary third-party guides.
3. Never post passwords, login details, private file paths, access tokens, account identifiers or SavedVariables.
4. Avoid executable attachments. Shared route packages will be validated **data-only** files, not scripts.
5. Mark unverified quests/endpoints clearly. Forever content can differ from Vanilla/Classic.
6. Moderators may remove spam, unsafe files or content violating platform rules.
